Jieun Kim

Investigating Trustworthy Machine Learning, Model Reliability & Robustness, Representation Learning, and Brain-Inspired AI

I am a Machine Learning Researcher focusing on Trustworthy and Brain-Inspired AI, spanning explainable time-series anomaly detection, continuous-time neural representation, and robust computational dynamics. My research background includes modeling Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs) at the Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST) advised by Dr. Taegon Kim, developing unsupervised Neural ODE frameworks for irregular telemetry at the Georgia Institute of Technology (Atlanta, GA, USA), and engineering distribution-guided Transformers for missing value imputation during my Master's at Korea University. Across these domains, my goal is to build reliable, explainable, and physics-guided AI systems for real-world decision making.

I received my M.S. in Industrial and Management Engineering from Korea University advised by Prof. Jun-Geol Baek, and hold a B.S. in Statistics & Computer Science from Sookmyung Women's University advised by Prof. Young-Geun Choi.

Unsupervised Neural ODE Modeling

라벨이 없는(Unlabeled) 비연속 센서 데이터 환경에서 인위적 임퓨테이션 전처리 없이 Neural ODEs(연속시간 미분방정식)를 도입하여 원인 오차 및 Point Anomaly 스파이크를 원본 그대로 자율 감지하는 시스템 구축.

Technical & Derived Feature Engineering

  • 물리적 파생 변수 수식화 (Physical Feature Derived Equations):
    • Supply/Return 온도차: $\Delta T_t = T_{\text{return}, t} - T_{\text{supply}, t}$
    • 유량 대비 열 전달비: $\text{Heat Flow Ratio}_t = \frac{\Delta T_t}{\text{Flow}_t} = \frac{T_{\text{return}, t} - T_{\text{supply}, t}}{\text{Flow}_t}$
  • Neural ODEs & SRE Scoring: 비연속 시계열 데이터를 억지로 보정 전처리하지 않고 연속시간 궤적으로 딥러닝 미분 모델링하여 원본 데이터 보존. SRE Scoring 기반 자율 이상 감지.
Neural ODEs Continuous-Time Dynamics Unsupervised Learning Unlabeled Irregular Series Performer Enc-Dec SRE Anomaly Scoring
